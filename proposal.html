1. Executive Summary & Solution Alignment

Following our formal commercial discussion, this proposal confirms PAROT Corp's capability to deliver a carrier-grade, highly scalable Smart Waste and Fleet Management platform engineered specifically for NEXORA's regional commercial expansion across Egypt, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the GCC.

The platform has been demonstrated in full operation via the live interactive demo. It fulfills all 11 requirements requested by NEXORA, including protocol agnosticism (Dingtek DF703 and MQTT), full multi-tenancy, white-label delivery under NEXORA's brand, complete source code handover, 90-day post-handover warranty, and a revised 30% / 30% / 40% milestone payment structure.

Strategic Objective: Empower NEXORA to market, sublicense, and operate its own proprietary-branded Smart Waste platform across master-planned cities, residential developments, and municipalities without vendor lock-in or recurring per-tenant royalties.

5. Acceptance Test Plan (ATP) Scenarios

Phase 1: Core Platform, Multi-Tenancy & Sensor Ingestion

Test ID Test Scenario Target Acceptance Criteria
ATP-1.1 Multi-Tenant Data Isolation Create two client tenants. Verify complete separation; Tenant A cannot query or view Tenant B data.
ATP-1.2 White-Label Theming Apply custom logo, hex palette (`#059669`), title, and localized Arabic/English login carousel.
ATP-1.3 Dingtek DF703 4G Ingestion Transmit live hex TCP socket packet (`0x80 ... 0x81`). Correctly decodes distance, fill %, temp, angle, battery.
ATP-1.4 MQTT Telemetry Integration Publish JSON payload over MQTT broker (EMQX). Telemetry ingested and reflected in real-time asset state.
ATP-1.5 Geospatial Map Visualization Render 200 pins color-coded by fill status (<50% green, 50-79% yellow, ≥80% red) with sub-second reload.
ATP-1.6 Critical Alarm Thresholds Simulate tilt >45° and heat >65°C. System raises instantaneous fire/tilt alarm and notification.
